## Cloud-native development

...is an approach to building and running applications that fully leverage cloud computing environments. Here are some key aspects:

- **Microservices Architecture**: Applications are broken down into small, independent services that can be developed, deployed, and scaled independently.

- **Containers**: These are used to package microservices and their dependencies, ensuring consistency across different environments.

- **DevOps and Continuous Delivery**: Emphasizes automation in building, testing, and deploying applications, enabling frequent and reliable updates.

- **Scalability and Resilience**: Cloud-native applications are designed to scale easily and handle failures gracefully.

- **APIs and Service Meshes**: APIs facilitate communication between microservices, while service meshes manage this communication efficiently.
